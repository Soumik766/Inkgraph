# Sample (author's voice)

Rain on the tiles. He counted the drops because counting was easier than thinking.

"You came back," she said.

"I said I would."

She didn't answer. The lamp guttered, and somewhere below the river kept its own counsel. He set the knife on the table, blade toward himself, the way his father had taught him. A man who points steel at a host has already decided how the night ends.

"Is it done?" she asked.

"It's done."

She poured the tea. Neither of them drank it.
