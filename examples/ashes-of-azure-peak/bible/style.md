# Style bible — Ashes of Azure Peak

## Narration
- Close third on Lin Feng, past tense. The narrator never knows more than Feng.
- Attitude: cold, observant, unsentimental. Emotion is shown through objects and hands, never named.

## Sentences & paragraphs (measured with `ink style`)
- avg sentence ~7 words; >50% of sentences ≤ 6 words. One long sentence per scene, at most.
- Paragraphs short (≈10–25 words). One-line paragraphs for hits.

## Dialogue
- Tags: "said"/"asked" only; mostly no tag at all.
- People answer a different question than the one asked.

## Scenes
- Fights: sensory, short, no technique names explained.
- Chapters end on a concrete image, not a statement.

## Banned
- em dashes in narration, "-ly" adverbs on dialogue tags, any named emotion ("he felt fear").
