# Chapter 6: The Steps

"My uncle kept something for you," Mo Qing said. "Not the pendant. A letter."

"I never saw a letter."

"No. Wei Shan burned the desk with the body."

They stood on the steps until the bell rang. Mo Qing did not draw his sword.

"I was wrong about the tea," he said, and walked away.

Feng watched him go. He did not tell him about the ledger.
