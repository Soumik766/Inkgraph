# Chapter 3: Ash on the River

Feng dreamed that Elder Mo walked into the courtyard and smiled at him. He woke to Mo Qing's blade at his throat.

"Where is it?"

"Where is what?"

Mo Qing pressed until the skin opened. Then he left.

At the Black River a ferry woman sang over the water. Old Hua, the boatmen called her. The jade pendant lay in her basket among the river stones, crest up.

The crest was the seal of the Mo Clan.
