# Chapter 1: Cold Pills

Lin Feng swept the Medicine Hall at dawn. Elder Mo watched from the doorway and said nothing.

Feng's thumb found the jade pendant under his robe. The crack was warm. It was always warm.

Mo Qing laughed from the steps. He flicked a pill at Feng's feet. It rolled and stopped against the broom.

"Eat," Mo Qing said. "You look hungry."

Feng swept around it.

Su Yan passed with her maids and did not look at either of them. Below the Azure Sect the Black River ran grey with the morning's ash.
