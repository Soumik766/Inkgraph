# Chapter 8: Heartroot

Wei Shan came to the inner court with a tray of pills for the Sect Master. Feng took the tray from him at the gate.

"I'll carry it," Feng said.

He smelled it before he saw it. Black heartroot, ground fine, rolled into the third pill.

He carried the tray to the garden. At the pond he stumbled. Six pills went into the water and the koi took them.

By evening the koi were floating. Wei Shan had already left the sect.
