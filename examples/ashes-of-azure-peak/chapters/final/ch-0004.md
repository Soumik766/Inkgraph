# Chapter 4: Foundation

Feng went down to the Black River at dusk. The ferry was tied up and empty.

He sat on the ice and breathed until his lungs burned. Something broke open behind his ribs. The river ice cracked in a ring around him.

Foundation. Alone, with blood on the snow.

Su Yan found him there. She looked at the blood, then at the ring in the ice.

"You should go back," she said.

She did not tell anyone. Old Hua's ferry was gone by morning.
