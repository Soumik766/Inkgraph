# Chapter 7: The Sect Master's Garden

The Sect Master summoned the outer disciples to his garden to choose a servant for the inner court. He chose Lin Feng.

Su Yan stood behind her father and looked at the koi.

"Foundation at seventeen," the Sect Master said, quietly, so only Feng heard. "And nobody noticed. Interesting."

Wei Shan bowed lowest of all when the choice was announced.
