# Chapter 2: The Elder's Cup

Lin Feng brewed the evening tea in the Medicine Hall, as he did every night. Elder Mo drank it at his desk.

He did not wake.

By morning the jade pendant was gone from Feng's neck. The cord lay on his mat, cut clean.

Mo Qing stood over his uncle's body for a long time. Then he looked at Feng.

Wei Shan, the new steward, arrived before the elders did. Wei Shan ordered the body burned by noon. Wei Shan did not ask who had made the tea.
