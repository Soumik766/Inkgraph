# Chapter 5: Ledgers

Wei Shan kept the Medicine Hall ledgers in a locked cabinet. Feng had swept beside it for three years.

The lock was cheap. The ledgers were not. Somebody had bought black heartroot in the name of Elder Mo, twice, the week before he died.

The signature was Wei Shan's.

Feng put the page back exactly as he had found it. Then he went to sweep the steps.

Mo Qing was waiting there.
